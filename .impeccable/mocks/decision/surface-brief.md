## Direction contract

### THESIS
Treats dosa circularity not as a standard SaaS card utility, but as sacred radial symmetry evaluated by an unyielding high-order computational metrology laboratory. Refuses generic dark-mode cyberpunk neon tropes, floating random particles, and stock dashboard grids in favor of an authentic seasoned cast-iron coordinate stage.

### OWN-WORLD
Seasoned cast-iron anthracite base (#101216, #161920) with chalk-dust ivory geometry lines (#fdfbf7, rgba(253,251,247,0.18)), warm golden ghee highlights (#f59e0b, #d97706), and red wax calibration seals (#b91c1c). Features concentric polar degree markings (0°–360°), micrometer vernier scales, and Kolam corner registration marks.

### STORY
The visitor immediately encounters the National Metrology Directorate for Dosa Circularity. They realize with delight that their culinary efforts are subjected to serious geometric inspection, upload an image or test a sample, watch calibration reticles lock on, and receive an authoritative verdict.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
A dominant circular seasoned-iron tawa measuring reticle occupying the center-top stage, flanked by academic status badges ('Nobel Prize Pending', 'Calibration Grade: Class 0'). Concentric polar degree lines surround the live inspection viewport, with immediate dropzone action and sample triggers.
