# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary Users**: South Indian food lovers, home cooks, engineers, developers, and internet humor enthusiasts.
- **Situation**: Cooking at home or dining out, taking a photo of a dosa on the tawa or plate to test its geometry.
- **Core Job**: Receive an instant, hilarious, mock-academic evaluation of their dosa's circular perfection to enjoy and share with family and friends.

## Product Purpose

The Dosa Circularity Analyzer™ provides playful entertainment by applying absurdly exaggerated scientific rigor to the traditional art of making dosas. Success is measured by user delight, laughter, and high shareability.

## Positioning

The world's foremost (and entirely satirical) computational geometry lab dedicated to dosa evaluation. Distinguishes itself by marrying genuine South Indian culinary touchstones (Amma's exacting standards, sambar surface physics) with deadpan laboratory parody ("Nobel Prize Pending", high-order mathematical jargon over randomized `Math.random()` results).

## Operating Context

- Responsive modern web application across desktop and mobile browsers.
- Fast, zero-friction client-side workflow: image upload/drop, animated diagnostic scan, instant score computation, and visual metric breakdown.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Confirmed Capabilities**:
  - Client-side image upload, dropzone, and preview.
  - Multi-step metrological radar scan and diagnostic sequence.
  - On-device local U-2-Net neural segmentation with boundary contour extraction.
  - Real polar metrology metrics: Isoperimetric Circularity Quotient ($4\pi A / P^2$), Radial Variance $\sigma$, Radial Max Deviation, Sambar Meniscus Stability, and Amma Critical Approval index.
  - Interactive contour tracing overlay and binary segmentation mask inspector.
  - Pure CSS animated dosa art and interactive particle canvas.
  - Satirical scientific news ticker.
- **Durable Constraints**:
  - 100% client-side on-device inference via WebAssembly/WebGPU; user images are never uploaded to remote servers.
  - Next.js / React / TypeScript / Tailwind CSS stack.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name**: Dosa Circularity Analyzer™ ("Nobel Prize Pending").
- **Voice & Tone**: High-tech scientific laboratory parody. Ultra-serious academic language, faux research institute citations, hyperbolic claims, and warm South Indian cultural wit.
- **Personality**: Intellectually absurd, culturally affectionate, engaging, and deadpan.

## Evidence on Hand

- Next.js application codebase located in `dosa-analyzer/`.
- Component library in `components/` (`Analyzer.tsx`, `DosaCSSArt.tsx`, `Hero.tsx`, `Navbar.tsx`, `ParticleCanvas.tsx`, `ResultsPanel.tsx`, `Ticker.tsx`, `UploadPanel.tsx`).
- Curated mock metrics, tiers, and fictional leaderboards in `lib/constants.ts`.
- Absence note: All leaderboard competitors, scientific papers, and ticker breaking news are entirely fictitious by design.

## Product Principles

1. **Commit to the Bit**: Maintain the deadpan academic research tone rigorously across all copy, tooltips, and data visualizations.
2. **Celebrate Culinary Truth**: Root the humor in real cultural rituals (Amma's critical gaze, the crisp golden edge, sambar dipping mechanics) rather than generic tech tropes.
3. **Instant Delights**: The path from photo upload to comprehensive "diagnostic report" must feel energetic, crisp, and responsive with rewarding micro-interactions.
4. **Privacy & Simplicity by Default**: Zero image uploads, zero logins, and zero tracking — pure browser-based fun.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Keyboard navigability across upload dropzone, interactive controls, and modal views.
- Strict adherence to `prefers-reduced-motion` for scanning bars and particle backgrounds.
- High-contrast typography and clear ARIA descriptions for metric progress bars and status badges.
